# The Head

> *"Hold the gate until the bells. After the bells, it won't matter."*
> — Warden-Commander Ilse Varga, the last night of the Siege of Coldharbor

The Head holds the Seat of **command and decision**: the captain, high priest, don, cell leader, guildmaster, or elected chair. The Head doesn't have to be the boss on paper — plenty of factions keep a figurehead while the real Head stands behind the throne. In play, the Head trades in tempo: their features make other people act sooner and better.

> Holding the Head's Seat is a role your *character* plays inside the fiction. It is not a licence to run the table. See [a note on the Head, and on playing together](characters/creation.md#a-note-on-the-head).

## Core Feature — Chain of Command

Once per conflict, issue a direct order to an ally who can perceive you. They may immediately pursue that goal with an edge. This doesn't consume their exchange.

## Faction Synergy

Each [faction turn](faction/faction-turn.md), the faction takes **one additional action — and the Head must take it personally.** (Two Heads make it +2, each taking one of the extra actions.) The Head lends no edge to anyone else's work, so the power is reach, not force: more gets done because the Head is doing it.

## Seat Features (start with two)

| Feature | Effect |
| --- | --- |
| **Read the Board** | Once per session, lay out a course of action you're weighing and roll any stat that fits how you're assessing it, against 3. **Win:** the GM tells you honestly how the path looks from where you stand — the likely gain, the likely cost, the thing you hadn't accounted for. **Triumph:** and names the one factor that would change the answer entirely. **Loss:** the board is clouded; you learn nothing you can act on. **Disaster:** you read it confidently and wrong, and the GM tells you something reassuring that isn't true. |
| **Hold the Line** | Your rally goals may target every ally in the conflict with a single contest, clearing morale conditions (*Shaken*, *Routed*, *Panicked*) from all of them on a win. |
| **Delegation** | Once per faction turn, a faction action that has no edge on it gains one. You saw who wasn't being listened to. |
| **The Buck Stops Here** | Once per session, when an ally loses a contest, redirect the consequence to yourself — the strain, the condition, or the blame, as the narrative allows. |
| **War Council** | When you take the **Prepare** move, roll Mental against 3 instead of simply banking an edge. **Win:** bank a primed edge for two different people. **Triumph:** three people. **Loss:** no edge for anyone — the plan fell apart in the room. |
| **Face of Authority** | Edge on contests to command, negotiate, or intimidate from recognized rank — including parley with enemies who respect the office, if not the person. |

## Callings

### The Sovereign — *rules by right*

You embody the faction's legitimacy. Crowned, anointed, or simply undeniable — what you claim, you tend to keep.

**Signature — By Right:** once per session, lay public claim to something in your faction's reach that no one securely holds. Roll Ideological against a pool set by how contested the claim is.

- **Win:** you get it — 1 stock of whatever the claim would plausibly produce, or a temporary asset that serves you until the session ends.
- **Triumph:** an extra stock, or the asset comes stronger than you asked for.
- **Loss:** the claim is heard and refused.
- **Disaster:** a rival claims it instead, and gets it.

### The Strategist — *rules by plan*

You were three moves ahead before the scene started — but even good plans meet the dice.

**Signature — Contingency:** once per session, when you or an ally suffers a loss or a costly success — anything short of a [Disaster](core/contests.md#outcomes) — declare that you planned for exactly this, and roll Mental against a difficulty set by how large the save is. **Win:** the preparation was real and pays off in full — the stashed key, the repositioned ally, the second exit. **Tie:** it works, but it tips your hand. **Loss:** the plan didn't survive contact, and the moment is yours to weather alone. Disasters outrun even good plans.

### The Firstblade — *rules from the front*

You command by being first over the wall.

**Signature — Follow Me:** once per conflict, when an ally [aids](core/contests.md#aid) a goal you lead and you win it, they don't just watch you succeed — they take a primed edge into the next contest they lead themselves. Nothing teaches like standing next to it.
