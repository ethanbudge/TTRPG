# Glossary

Every term of art in The Party, in plain language. Terms in *italics* have their own entries.

**Accessibility** — How reachable a stock or asset is from where the characters are operating: **Ready** (usable now), **Reachable** (one goal or an hour away), **Remote** (elsewhere; time brings it closer), **Cut Off** (blocked until dealt with).

**Advance** — Any permanent improvement earned by finishing a *project*: a new feature, a new gift, a raised stat, or something built for the *faction*.

**Agenda** — The faction's cause in motion: the milestone *countdowns* the campaign is currently pursuing.

**Aid** — Spending your own turn to help an ally's roll instead of pursuing your own goal. One helper, one *edge*, said out loud — and they share the consequences.

**Arm** — The *Seat* of building and repair: engineer, artificer, wright, sapper, shipwright.

**Asset** — Faction property that can act: a strike team, a spy ring, a gunboat. Has a rating (its dice), 3 strain boxes, and a place on the map.

**Band** — One of four distances: **Contact, Close, Far, Distant**. Sized relative to the arena — a band spans a room in a brawl and a star system in a fleet action.

**Broken** — Out of the conflict: your last strain box is marked. The winning side says what it looks like, within the stakes. Under mortal stakes, the player chooses between the scar and the exit.

**Calling** — One of three styles within each *Seat*, granting a signature feature.

**Channel** — Acting through the faction in a conflict: rolling a faction stat or an *asset's* rating instead of your own dice.

**Cohesion** — The faction's will to remain one thing. Starts at 5 and drains; at 0, *Crisis*.

**Commit** — Spending 1 Ready *stock* for one Faction Resource *edge*.

**Condition** — A named state — *Blinded, Shaken, Starving* — that imposes one *snag* on any roll it would plausibly hinder. **Hard conditions** also forbid a category of action until cleared.

**Conflict** — Structured scene time — *exchanges*, *goals*, *contests* — used when several actors urgently want incompatible things. The same procedure for a fistfight or a trade war.

**Contest** — Every roll in the game: your pool against an opposing pool, higher sum wins, ties go to the acting side at a cost.

**Costly success** — A tied contest: the acting side gets what it wanted, and pays a real cost for it.

**Countdown** — A track of segments (2, 4, 6, or 8) for anything too big for one roll. Wins strike segments off, Triumphs strike two; at zero, the thing it names happens. **Situation countdowns** run inside a scene; **[project](core/contests.md#project-countdowns) countdowns** are the long work of *faction turns*.

**Crisis** — What happens when *Cohesion* hits 0: a schism, mutiny, or purge, played out at full length.

**Disaster** — Losing a contest by 8 or more. The stakes land hard, and harm marks 2 strain.

**Downtime move** — A *personal move* a character may take on the *faction turn* instead of a faction action: Prepare or Recover, or a personal *project* — Improve, Strengthen, Specialize, Diversify, or Acquire.

**Edge / Snag** — One die added to (edge) or removed from (snag) a pool. Pools stay between 1 and 5; extra edges past 5 strip dice from the opposition.

**Exchange** — One round of a *conflict*. Every actor gets one *goal* per exchange. How much time it covers is the conflict's clockspeed: **Blitz** (seconds), **Operation** (minutes–hours), **Season** (days–weeks), **Era** (months+).

**Faction** — The organization the player characters belong to. Shared by the whole table; has stats, a Ledger, holdings, a gift, a flaw, and *Cohesion*.

**Faction turn** — The twenty-odd minutes when the faction earns (Income), pays its *Keep*, and each player takes one move — a faction action or a *downtime move*. Runs whenever the GM lets real time pass.

**Fist** — The *Seat* of force and protection: champion, enforcer, bodyguard, siege-breaker.

**Heel** — The *Seat* of movement and reconnaissance: scout, ranger, pilot, navigator, messenger.

**Flaw** — A gift in reverse: a permanent, narrow *snag* with a life of its own. Characters start with one; *scars* add more. The faction has one too.

**GM (Game Master)** — The player who describes the world, plays every NPC and rival faction, sets *stakes*, and rolls the opposing dice in every contest.

**Gift** — A positive trait open to any character: one narrow edge, permission, or trick. Characters start with two; the faction takes one.

**Goal** — What you declare on your turn: one sentence saying what you want to be true. The table finds the dice from there.

**Holding** — A place that is genuinely the faction's: a chapel, safehouse, dry dock, or moon base. One line on the sheet — what it gives, what it asks.

**Immediate action** — A *faction turn* move that resolves in one roll, as opposed to a *project*.

**Inadequate Leadership** — The *snag* you take for leading or aiding a faction action rolled on a stat you personally rate 1 or 2.

**Keep** — The faction's cost of existing: the short list of needs settled at each *faction turn*. A **consumed need** is spent each turn (rations); a **standing need** must merely be present (a garrison's loyalty). Unmet needs mean [going hungry](faction/resources.md#going-hungry).

**Ledger** — The faction sheet's list of every *stock* it owns — type, place, and *accessibility*.

**Personal move** — See *downtime move*.

**Primed** — An edge prepared for a specific character ahead of time — by an ally's set-up goal, by a Prepare move on the *faction turn*, or by a feature that banks one. One at a time, and it expires when the conflict (or the job) ends.

**Project** — A *countdown* started and struck on *faction turns*, using the one move each turn gives you. All advancement, construction, and long campaigns are projects. Starting one takes a move but no roll; striking one takes a move and a contest; you may only have one of your own running at a time.

**Protection tag** — Armor, cover, or its social equivalent: once per conflict, negate 1 incoming *strain*.

**Scar** — The permanent price of surviving being *broken* under mortal stakes: a new *flaw*, and a slow return.

**Scale tier** — Personal (people), Company (squads, vessels), or Host (armies, fleets). Harming a larger tier takes leverage.

**Scene** — One continuous piece of the story in one place and time. The GM calls when a scene ends.

**Seat** — Your role in the faction: Head, Mouth, Fist, Eye, Heel, Mind, Arm, Heart, or Spine. Describes your place in the organization, never your day job.

**Standing need** — A *Keep* need that must be present but is never spent (a garrison's loyalty, a shrine's holiness); it fails only when that stock leaves.

**Session** — One sitting of play, typically 2–4 hours.

**Session zero** — The first gathering, where the table builds the faction and characters together.

**Stakes** — What happens if the acting side loses a contest. Always stated before dice are rolled.

**Stat** — One of four ratings from 1 to 5: **Physical, Mental, Emotional, Ideological**. The rating is the dice pool. Factions have the same four.

**Stock** — One unit of a faction resource, sitting somewhere real on the map. Eight types: an internal and external pool for each stat.

**Strain** — The game's harm: boxes marked when you lose a contest where harm is on the line. Five boxes for people; fewer for mooks and assets. Clears with a real breather.

**Triumph** — Winning a contest by 8 or more: the goal happens *and* the winner picks a boon.

**Zoom** — Changing a conflict's scale mid-stream — duel to riot, boarding action to fleet battle — carrying all strain, conditions, and countdowns along.
